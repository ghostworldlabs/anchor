    Wave 9 · Multi-Provider Calendar & Email Connectors · OS Credential Vault

    Your Calendar & Email connectors on the mother ship

    Connect Google / Gmail, Microsoft 365 / Outlook, or any Custom / Institutional IMAP & CalDAV/ICS feed. Credentials are stored exclusively in the Windows OS Credential Vault (advapi32) — never in JSON files on disk. Sync is strictly read-only and reconciles correspondence and calendar deadlines directly against your active project roadmaps.

    🛡 Read-Only & OS Vault Guardrail: Anchor reads envelope headers and ICS calendar windows to reconcile project milestones (e.g. Family Trusts, MBA Teaching AI, Campaign). It never sends email or modifies external calendar events without your explicit approval.

    📧 Google / Gmail + Calendar

    Preset · Port 993

    Connect Gmail IMAP (imap.gmail.com:993) with a 16-character App Password or OAuth token, plus your private Google Calendar ICS feed.

    Connect Outlook / Office 365 (outlook.office365.com:993) and published Exchange / Outlook ICS calendar feeds.
