Research-only efforts just stop and stay. An effort started at Research that isn't advanced (e.g. Fuel-cladding material survey) keeps only its Research node lit and waits in the list. Hit Advance → Plan to light the next node in the same session.
Everything is auto-saved per stage — no save button. Each reached stage persists its docs, summary, and (for a finished Build) its deliverable; the row's right cluster links straight to them. Every effort is reopenable. Killing a row (⋯ → Kill) sends its content to this project's Boneyard, where the docs & summary stay searchable.
Why one row per effort
Unlike a bin/column board, an effort never migrates. It stays put and its inline track fills left-to-right — making the trio's continuity literal: research → plan → build, one continuous session.
Mockup only — nothing here is wired. This is Layout B (“Effort Track”) for visual approval. Buttons, links, segments, and the dropdown are non-functional placeholders illustrating the one-row-per-effort, fill-in-the-track concept.
