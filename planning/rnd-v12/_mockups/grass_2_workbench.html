For a 15 MWt core, hour-1 decay heat ≈ 300 kWt. Driving head comes from
the density difference over the riser. With a 6 m elevation between core
centerline and the sink, and ΔT ≈ 40 °C across the loop:
 
  ṁ ≈ Q / (c_p · ΔT) ≈ 300 / (4.18 · 40) ≈ 1.8 kg/s
  → a single 4″ loop carries it with margin. Single sink is viable at hour-1.
