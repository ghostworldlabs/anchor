The rules at a glance: An effort is one continuous session. Research-only efforts live on the left; clicking Advance to Plan → migrates the tile to the right, carrying its research docs + summary as lineage. On the right, Plan and Build share one tile and the badge auto-advances. There is no save button — every effort always auto-saves its documents, deliverables, and a generated summary, and every tile (including finished ones) is reopenable.
