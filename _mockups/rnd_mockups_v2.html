Smart-Home Protocol Survey for Multi-Agent Control

researchPrime · Agentic-Home · v3 · 2026-06-05

1 · Latency model

We model end-to-end actuation latency as the sum of broker, inference, and device-response terms. For a fleet of n agents contending on a single MQTT broker, expected delay is bounded by:

Ltotal = Lnet + Σi=1n ( tinfer,i ) + λ·√n

where λ captures broker queueing under concurrent publishes. The √n term dominates beyond ~12 concurrent agents, motivating a sharded-broker topology.
