Proposal B — Full-width backlog board (its own tab)

Grass becomes its own full-width tab (not a narrow lane) — a sortable backlog table: idea · source · status · id · last activity · actions. Filter by status (raw / refined / promoted). Clicking a row drops a develop panel inline. Best when you have many ideas and want to manage them like a backlog.
